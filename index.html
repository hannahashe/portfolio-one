About me

I’m interested in the space where design, accessibility, content and development meet. My background in English Literature, Philosophy, communications and cooperative operations gives me a strong foundation in clear thinking, user empathy and practical problem-solving. Alongside development, I bring experience in visual art, illustration, writing and editing, which helps me approach digital projects with an eye for both visual identity and content clarity.

Since completing a full-stack development bootcamp, I’ve been building projects that focus on thoughtful interfaces, real user needs and systems that make information easier to understand and act on.
